# LTX Desktop Templates

LTX 更适合长一点、具体一点的 prompt。建议写成完整场景，不只写关键词。

## 通用 LTX 模板

```text
A [STYLE] video set in [SETTING]. [SUBJECT DESCRIPTION]. [ACTION SEQUENCE IN 2-3 STEPS]. The camera starts with [START SHOT], then [CAMERA MOVEMENT], ending on [FINAL FRAMING]. Lighting is [LIGHTING]. The atmosphere is [MOOD]. Details include [MATERIALS, BACKGROUND MOTION, MICRO ACTIONS]. No subtitles, no on-screen text, no watermark.
```

## 产品生活方式镜头

```text
A warm lifestyle product video set in a real [ROOM OR LOCATION]. [TARGET USER] places [PRODUCT] on [SURFACE], uses it naturally, and the benefit becomes visually clear. The camera begins with a medium handheld shot, then slowly pushes into a detailed close-up of the product. Soft natural light, realistic home atmosphere, clean composition, subtle background movement, premium but authentic commercial style. No subtitles, no on-screen text, no watermark, keep the product shape consistent.
```

## 高质感广告镜头

```text
A premium studio commercial for [PRODUCT]. The product sits on [SURFACE] with [MATERIAL DETAILS]. [SIMPLE MOTION OR REVEAL] happens slowly and elegantly. The camera performs a smooth orbit from a three-quarter angle into a macro close-up. Controlled softbox lighting, crisp reflections, dark neutral background, luxury color grading, realistic materials, no text, no logo, no watermark, no distorted packaging.
```

## 短剧情镜头

```text
A cinematic short scene in [LOCATION]. [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION] notices [PROBLEM OR EVENT], reacts with [MICRO EXPRESSION], then performs [ONE CLEAR ACTION]. The camera starts as a medium shot and slowly pushes in to a close-up. Natural lighting, realistic acting, subtle background details, filmic color grading, no subtitles, no text overlays, no watermark.
```

## 首尾帧/连续镜头写法

如果要做连续视频，每个镜头都重复关键角色和服装：

```text
The same [CHARACTER], wearing [CONSISTENT CLOTHING], in [CONTINUED LOCATION]. This shot continues from the previous moment. [NEW ACTION]. The camera maintains [CAMERA STYLE] and the lighting remains [LIGHTING]. Keep the character appearance, outfit, and environment consistent. No subtitles, no text, no watermark.
```

## 适合 LTX 的详细字段

- 角色外观: 年龄、发型、服装、姿态。
- 动作节奏: first, then, finally。
- 镜头路径: starts with, slowly pushes, ends on。
- 光线: soft window light, neon reflection, golden hour, controlled studio lighting。
- 氛围: calm, urgent, premium, cozy, mysterious。
- 材质: brushed metal, matte plastic, glossy ceramic, linen fabric, wet asphalt。
