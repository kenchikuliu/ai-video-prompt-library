# Shot Taxonomy

Use this file as a quick menu when building prompts.

## Shot Types

- macro close-up: product texture, food, small object, material detail.
- close-up: face, hands, product hero detail.
- medium shot: person using product, lifestyle scene.
- wide shot: location, travel, city, establishing scene.
- overhead shot: cooking, desk setup, flat lay, workflow.
- low-angle shot: power, scale, product hero, dramatic subject.
- over-the-shoulder shot: screen use, tutorial, discovery moment.
- point-of-view shot: first-person experience, adventure, tool usage.

## Camera Movement

- slow push-in: premium reveal, emotional focus, product hero.
- smooth orbit: product showcase, object inspection.
- handheld follow: UGC, documentary, realistic social media.
- pan left / pan right: reveal comparison, before-after.
- tilt down: reveal object on table, environment-to-product transition.
- dolly back: reveal full scene, scale, surprise.
- static locked shot: clean product demo, satisfying transformation.
- drone-like glide: travel, landscape, architecture.

## Lighting

- soft window light: lifestyle, home, authentic product.
- golden hour: travel, warmth, emotional scene.
- neon reflections: urban, tech, cyberpunk, night hook.
- controlled softbox: premium studio product.
- low-key lighting: mystery, luxury, dramatic story.
- bright clean daylight: UGC, kitchen, tutorial, education.
- backlight with haze: cinematic silhouette, atmosphere.

## Styles

- premium product commercial
- authentic handheld UGC
- cinematic short film
- realistic documentary
- clean educational explainer
- cozy lifestyle
- futuristic technology visual
- travel documentary
- food commercial
- minimal studio aesthetic

## Motion Verbs

- rises slowly
- rotates gently
- slides into frame
- opens smoothly
- transforms gradually
- reflects light
- reveals hidden detail
- moves through mist
- pours slowly
- clicks into place
- folds neatly
- glides across the surface

## Strong First Frame Ideas

- an unexpected object in a normal scene
- a messy-before state
- a dramatic close-up with steam, rain, sparks, or reflection
- a hand about to reveal the product
- a face reacting to a surprising result
- a tiny miniature world on a common object
- a split composition showing contrast
